Master Services Agreement

between Northbridge Analytics Ltd ("Provider") and the Customer named in the Order Form ("Customer"), dated 1 May 2026.

1. Definitions

Service
means the hosted analytics platform identified in the Order Form, excluding any third-party data feeds.
Confidential Information
means any information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, but excluding information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party.
Term
means the initial twelve-month period beginning on the Effective Date, together with any renewal periods.

2. Provision of services

  1. The Provider shall make the Service available to the Customer for the duration of the Term. General improvements to the provider's service offerings outside the Order Form are not included.
  2. The Customer is responsible for the security of its own login credentials and for any use of the service by its personnel.

3. Confidentiality

  1. Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it except as permitted under this agreement.
  2. This clause does not apply to information that is not confidential information, such as marketing materials each party makes public.

This agreement, together with the Order Form, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
