Why automated tools miss this: a read-only <textarea> with a proper <label> and aria-describedby passes every form-label and name check. The tab characters are invisible to a linter and perfectly valid. No checker can perceive that the tabs render three aligned columns (Name / Title / Phone) per person, so none can detect that a screen reader reads each row's three fields as one run-on string with the tab stops collapsed away.
