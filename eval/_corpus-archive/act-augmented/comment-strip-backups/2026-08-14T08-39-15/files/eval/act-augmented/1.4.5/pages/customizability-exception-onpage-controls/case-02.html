About the exhibition

9 March – 28 July · Main Hall, Level 2

Drawing on the Brandt collection and key loans, Bauhaus in Colour traces how the school’s painters, weavers and stage designers used colour as a structural element rather than decoration. The show gathers Itten’s colour-wheel studies, Albers’ glass assemblages and a reconstruction of the 1923 stage workshop.
