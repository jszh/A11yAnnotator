Cedar Ridge Park — Trail Conditions

Updated this morning by the ranger station.

The park is open daily from dawn to dusk, and the three trailheads (North Gate, Cedar Loop, and the Overlook spur) all share the main parking lot off Route 9.

Before you hike

  1. Sign in at the kiosk so rangers know you are on the trail.
  2. Check the posted fire-danger flag before lighting any stove.
  3. Fill water bottles at the pump; there is no water past the second mile.
  4. Start back at least two hours before dusk.

What the trail markers mean

White blaze
Main loop. Follow these to return to the parking lot.
Blue blaze
Connector trail to an adjacent loop or viewpoint.
Double blaze
A turn is coming; the upper mark points the direction.
Red blaze
Closed or hazardous section. Do not proceed.
