COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) is made and entered into as of March 14, 2022, by and between Harborview Properties LLC (“Landlord”) and Brightline Logistics, Inc. (“Tenant”).

1. Premises. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the warehouse and adjoining office space located at 1480 Cargo Way, Suite 200, Oakland, California 94607 (the “Premises”).

2. Term. The term of this Lease shall be sixty (60) months, commencing April 1, 2022, and ending March 31, 2027, unless sooner terminated as provided herein.
