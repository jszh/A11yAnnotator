Spiced Tomato Relish

Makes 6 jars · 90 minutes · Water-bath canning

Method

Simmer the tomatoes, onion, vinegar and spices uncovered for about forty minutes, stirring often, until the relish mounds softly on a spoon and the liquid has reduced. Ladle the hot relish into sterilized jars, leaving 1 cm of headspace, then

do NOT seal the jars until the headspace has cooled to room temperature — sealing while hot traps steam, prevents a proper vacuum, and can cause spoilage or jar failure. Once cooled, wipe the rims, apply the lids, and process in a boiling-water bath for 15 minutes.

Tip: Write the canning date on each lid with a grease pencil before you start — it is far easier than peeling a sticker off later, and the date helps you rotate your pantry.
