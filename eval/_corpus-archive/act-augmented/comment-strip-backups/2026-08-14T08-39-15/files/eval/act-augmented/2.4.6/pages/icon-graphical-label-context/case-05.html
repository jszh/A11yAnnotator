Smoky Charred Corn Ribs

By Dele Adeyemi · 35 min · Serves 4 · Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 ears fresh corn, husked
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp chipotle powder
  • Flaky salt and lime, to finish

Method

  1. Quarter each ear lengthwise through the cob to make four "ribs."
  2. Whisk butter with paprika and chipotle; brush over the corn.
  3. Char on a hot grill 8–10 minutes, turning, until edges curl and blacken.
  4. Finish with flaky salt and a squeeze of lime. Serve hot.
