Demo note (not shown to users): This page fails WCAG 2.4.6 Test 5.B. The "Betrag" (Amount) label names the topic but states none of its data requirements: the field accepts only NN,NN — a comma decimal, exactly two fraction digits, no € symbol, no thousands dot. The most natural German entry "85,00 €" is rejected because of the symbol, and the required shape is revealed only in the post-submit error. Contrast the sibling "IBAN (DE, 22 Stellen)" label, which DOES carry its format. Automated tools pass it: the German label is associated and non-empty, so axe-core/WAVE/Lighthouse see a properly labelled field. No tool reads the /^\d+,\d{2}$/ validator to learn the € symbol is rejected, and none can judge a locale-specific currency-format omission.
