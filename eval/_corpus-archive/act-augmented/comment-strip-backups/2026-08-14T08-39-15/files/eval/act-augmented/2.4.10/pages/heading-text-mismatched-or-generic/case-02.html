Shipping, Returns & Refunds

Everything you need to know about how we send your order, how to track it, and how refunds are handled.

Shipping & Delivery

We dispatch from our Leeds warehouse Monday to Friday. Standard delivery is £3.95 and arrives in three to five working days; orders over £60 ship free. Express delivery (£7.95) is dispatched the same day if you order before 1pm.

We currently ship to mainland UK and Northern Ireland only. The Highlands and island postcodes may take one to two extra working days.

Tracking your order

Once your parcel leaves the warehouse you'll receive an email with a Royal Mail or DPD tracking number. You can also view live status under My account → Orders. If tracking has not updated for 48 hours after dispatch, the carrier may have mislaid the parcel — contact us and we'll open a trace.

Shipping & Delivery

You may return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within five to seven working days of us receiving the returned goods.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging.
  • A £2.50 restocking charge is deducted from refunds on sale items.
  • We do not refund the original outbound delivery charge unless the item was faulty.
  • Gift-card purchases are refunded as store credit, never to a bank card.

Contact us

Our customer-care team is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm, on 0800 555 0142 or at care@loomwellhome.co.uk. Please have your order number ready.
