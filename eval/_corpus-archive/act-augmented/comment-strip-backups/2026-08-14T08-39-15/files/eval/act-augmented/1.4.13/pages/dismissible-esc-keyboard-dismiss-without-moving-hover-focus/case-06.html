EASTFIELD MUSEUM OF ASIAN ART · Gallery 7 — Lacquer & Inlay

Writing box (suzuribako)

Japan, Edo period, 18th century · Wood, lacquer, gold and silver maki-e ("sprinkled picture") — a decorative technique in which metal powder is sprinkled onto wet lacquer to form a design, then sealed under further coats and polished. Eastfield Museum, Gallery 7 didactics. inlay

This box held the ink stone, brushes, and water dropper used for calligraphy. Its lid depicts autumn grasses bending under a low moon, a seasonal motif prized by Edo collectors for its restraint.

The interior trays are fitted to the implements they carried, so each piece returns to its place when the box is closed.
