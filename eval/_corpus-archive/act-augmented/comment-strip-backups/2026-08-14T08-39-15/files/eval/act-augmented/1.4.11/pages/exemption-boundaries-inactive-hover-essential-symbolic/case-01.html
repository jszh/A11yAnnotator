Why automated tools miss this: Both buttons share the exact same faint palette (2px #c9c9c9 border ≈ 1.46:1 on white). A contrast scanner measures pixels, so it will treat the two buttons identically — either flagging both or, because many scanners skip the disabled subtree, skipping both. Neither is correct. The disabled Enroll in AutoPay button is exempt (inactive UI), but the operable Pay now button is NOT — its border is the only thing distinguishing the control from the page and it must reach 3:1. Deciding which faint button is in scope requires recognizing which one is actually operable, a behavioral/semantic judgement no pixel scan makes.
