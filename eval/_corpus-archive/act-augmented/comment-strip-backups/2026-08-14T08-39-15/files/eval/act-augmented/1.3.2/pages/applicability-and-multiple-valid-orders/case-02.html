Roasted on Tuesday, on your table by Friday

Cedar & Co. is a small-batch roastery on the harbour. We buy single lots, roast to order each week, and ship within forty-eight hours so the beans never sit on a shelf.

How the weekly roast works

Every Monday we cup the green coffee that landed that week and decide the roast curves. On Tuesday we run the batches; on Wednesday and Thursday we rest and bag them; on Friday the couriers collect. Order before Sunday midnight to make the coming Tuesday roast.

What’s on this week

This week’s lots are a washed Kenyan from Nyeri (blackcurrant, cane sugar) and a natural Ethiopian from Guji (blueberry, jasmine). Both are available as whole bean or ground for your brew method, in 250 g and 1 kg bags.

Subscriptions

A rolling subscription locks in the early-bird price and a rotating choice of lots. Pause or skip any week from your account before the Sunday cut-off; there is no minimum term.
