Cook mode: Slow-braised short ribs

Hands-on 25 min · total 3 hr 40 min · Tab through the steps and start a timer where you need one.

2 Sear brown ribs on all sides

Pat the short ribs dry and season. In a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high heat, sear the ribs in batches until deeply browned on every side, about 30 minutes total. Don't crowd the pan.

3 Sweat soften the aromatics

Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion, carrot, and celery to the rendered fat and cook, stirring, until softened and just golden, about 8 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste and garlic for the last minute.

4 Braise low oven, lid on

Return the ribs to the pot, add the stock and wine until two-thirds submerged, bring to a simmer, then cover and transfer to a 160 °C oven. Braise until fork-tender, about 3 hours, turning once at the halfway mark.
