Master Services Agreement

Between Northwind Traders, Inc. and Contoso Analytics LLC · Version 0.7 · Last edited 14 May 2026

1. Engagement

Contoso Analytics LLC ("Provider") agrees to furnish the data-engineering services described in Schedule A to Northwind Traders, Inc. ("Client") for the term set out in Section 4. Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with prevailing industry standards.

2. Fees and Payment

Client shall pay Provider the fees set out in Schedule B within thirty (30) days of receipt of a correct invoice. Late amounts accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using no less than a reasonable standard of care and shall not disclose it to third parties except as required to perform under this Agreement or as compelled by law.
