Resetting your password

Last updated 12 days ago · 2 min read

If you can't sign in because you've forgotten your password — or you think someone else may know it — you can reset it from the sign-in screen without contacting support. The link you receive is valid for 60 minutes.

Reset from the sign-in screen

  1. Go to the sign-in page and select Forgot your password? beneath the password field.
  2. Enter the email address on your account and choose Send reset link.
  3. Open the email titled "Reset your Cendre password" and select the button inside it. If it isn't in your inbox within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
  4. Enter a new password (at least 12 characters, with one number and one symbol) and confirm it.
  5. Select Save new password. You'll be signed in automatically on this device.
Didn't get the email? Make sure you used the exact address on your account. If your organization uses single sign-on, you reset your password with your identity provider, not here.

If you're already signed in

  1. Open Settings → Security.
  2. Select Change password, enter your current password, then your new one.
  3. Select Update. You'll be asked to sign in again on your other devices.

Trouble after resetting

If you reset your password but still can't sign in, you may be entering it on a cached page. Fully close and reopen your browser, or sign in from a private window. Still stuck? Contact support from the help center home page.
