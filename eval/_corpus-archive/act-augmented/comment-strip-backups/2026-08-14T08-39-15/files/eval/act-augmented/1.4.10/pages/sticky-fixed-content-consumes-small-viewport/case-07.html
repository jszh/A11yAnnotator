Planned engineering work this weekend

The Riverside Line will not run between Harbour Gate and Old Mill on Saturday and Sunday. Replacement buses will operate every 15 minutes. Allow extra time for your journey.

Riverside Line — Service Changes

Last updated: Friday 13 June, 4:10 p.m.

Weekend closure: Harbour Gate ↔ Old Mill closed for track renewal, Sat–Sun. Use replacement bus route R.

What is changing

To replace ageing track between Harbour Gate and Old Mill, trains will not serve those four stations this weekend. Trains will continue to run normally on the rest of the line. The work is part of a multi-year programme to reduce delays caused by speed restrictions over the older sections of track.

Refunds and accessibility

If your usual journey is disrupted, off-peak fares apply all weekend. Replacement buses are low-floor and wheelchair accessible. Staff will be on hand at Harbour Gate and Old Mill to assist passengers who need help transferring between rail and bus.

For real-time updates during the closure, check the live status page or sign up for line alerts by text. Normal Monday service resumes from first train.
