Why automated tools miss this (one toolbar, four different verdicts):
  • Delete ([disabled]): icon ~1.7:1 but the control is inactive → NA / exempt.
  • Draw (.tool--active-fail): same faint ~1.7:1 icon, but it is operable → its identifying icon must reach 3:1 → FAILS.
  • Zoom (.tool--glow): icon is ~7.6:1 (passes); the faint hover glow is supplemental → NA.
  • Close ✕ (.panel-close): a symbolic text character used as an icon at ~2.0:1 → non-text content that FAILS 3:1.
A contrast scanner measures pixels and would treat the two ~1.7:1 icons identically (Delete and Draw), exempt or flag the glow wrongly, and skip the ✕ as plain text. Only a human reasoning about disabled-vs-active, hover-vs-state, and symbol-vs-language can assign the correct per-element verdicts. In RTL the close glyph is also visually on the left, complicating naive position heuristics.
