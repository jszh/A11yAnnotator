Palletizing cells that pay for themselves in 14 months

The NW-720 palletizing cell drops into your existing line, handles mixed-case patterns up to 1,400 cases per hour, and ships pre-validated for food, beverage, and pharma duty cycles.

1,400cases / hour, mixed pattern
14 motypical payback period
99.4%uptime across deployed fleet

Most integrators quote a six-month installation. The NW-720 ships as a closed cell with the controller, safety scanners, and gripper pre-commissioned, so a typical line is palletizing within five working days. Pattern recipes are stored on the controller and recalled from the operator panel — no PC, no engineering visit.

Every cell is covered by a four-year parts warranty and a remote-diagnostics subscription that flags bearing wear and gripper-seal fatigue before they cause a line stop. Talk to a sales engineer about a duty-cycle assessment for your facility.
