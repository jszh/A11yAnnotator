Demo note (not shown to users): This page fails WCAG 2.4.6 Test 5.B. The legend establishes that "(required)" is the required-field cue. Two fields carry it; the genuinely required #email field does not, so its label "Email" fails to communicate the "required" data requirement that TT 5.B enumerates. Automated tools pass it: the label is associated and non-empty, the required attribute is present and even programmatically exposes aria-required semantics — so axe sees a labelled, required input with no error. No tool can judge that, given the page's own visible convention, the absent "(required)" word makes this label non-descriptive of its requirement.
