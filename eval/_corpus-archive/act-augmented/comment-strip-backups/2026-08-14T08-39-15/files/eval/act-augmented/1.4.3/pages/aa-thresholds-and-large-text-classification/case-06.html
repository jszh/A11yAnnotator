Returns & Refunds

Last updated 2 June

Unworn items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached. Sale items marked “final” and personalised goods are not eligible for return.

Refunds are processed within five business days of our warehouse receiving the returned parcel. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been issued; your bank may take a further few days to post it to your statement.

To start a return, sign in to your account and select the order, or reply to your order-confirmation email and our team will send a prepaid label.
