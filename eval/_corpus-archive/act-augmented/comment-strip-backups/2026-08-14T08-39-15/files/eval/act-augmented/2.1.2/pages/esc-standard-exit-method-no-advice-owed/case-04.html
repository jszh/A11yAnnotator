Essay 2 — Close reading

Due Fri 26 Jun · 1500 words · Plain-text or formatted submission accepted.

In the opening stanza, the poet's diction resists the reader's expectation of resolution…

Editing. Tab inserts indentation. To move focus out to the toolbar, press Alt+F10 (Option+F10 on macOS).

Why this is a PASS that requires human interpretation: Inside the editing area Tab is repurposed to insert indentation, so Tab does not move focus out — that is exactly the rich-text-editor situation in the WCAG Understanding. Because the release method (Alt+F10 / Option+F10) is not an unmodified arrow/Tab key, advice is owed — and the advice is present, shown when the area receives focus. Whether Alt+F10 counts as a "standard exit method" (the spec leaves this to authors/auditors) and whether the advice is adequate are human judgment calls; and a human must press the combo to confirm it actually releases focus. Automated tools neither read the significance of the instruction nor drive the keyboard.
