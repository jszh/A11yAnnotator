Brown-Butter Banana Bread

By Priya Sundaram · Prep 15 min · Bake 55 min · Makes 1 loaf

Browning the butter first gives this loaf a deep, nutty backbone that ordinary banana bread never reaches. Use bananas so ripe they look ruined — that is exactly when they are sweetest.

Ingredients

2 cups all-purpose flour
1 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp fine sea salt
1/2 cup unsalted butter, browned and cooled
3 very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/2 cups)
2 large eggs, room temperature

Method

  1. Heat the butter in a light-colored pan over medium until it foams, then smells nutty and turns amber, 5–7 minutes. Pour into a bowl and cool slightly.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl.
  3. Stir the mashed bananas and eggs into the browned butter, then fold in the dry ingredients until just combined.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 50–55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
