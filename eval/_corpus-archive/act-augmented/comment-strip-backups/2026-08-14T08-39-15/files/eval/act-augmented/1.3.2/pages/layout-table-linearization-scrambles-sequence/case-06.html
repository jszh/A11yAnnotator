How returns work

Three steps, ten minutes, free return shipping on every order.

1

Start your return

Open My Orders, pick the item, and choose a reason. You have 30 days from delivery.
2

Print the label

We email a prepaid label instantly. Print it and tape it to the original box (or any box you have).
3

Drop it off

Leave it at any SwiftShip locker or partner store. Your refund posts 3–5 business days after we scan it.

Questions? Chat with us 7 days a week, 8am–8pm.
