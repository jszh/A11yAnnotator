About the role

Northwind's Payments Platform team owns the services that move money between our customers, their suppliers, and the banking partners that settle our robotics-as-a-service contracts. As a Senior Backend Engineer you will design and operate the ledger, reconciliation, and payout services that process tens of millions of dollars each month, with correctness and auditability as first-class requirements.

What you'll do

  • Design and build idempotent, strongly-consistent payment APIs in Go backed by PostgreSQL.
  • Own the double-entry ledger service end to end: schema, migrations, reconciliation jobs, and on-call.
  • Integrate new banking and card-network partners, including webhook ingestion and settlement files.
  • Drive down P99 latency and improve the observability of the payout pipeline (metrics, tracing, alerting).
  • Mentor mid-level engineers and review designs for new payments features.

What we're looking for

  • 6+ years building backend services, with at least 2 on payments, ledgers, or other money-movement systems.
  • Deep comfort with relational data modeling, transactions, and consistency guarantees.
  • Experience operating production services on AWS with an on-call rotation.
  • A bias toward correctness, testing, and clear written design docs.

Compensation & benefits

Base salary $172,000–$208,000 depending on level and location, plus equity, 401(k) match, fully covered medical/dental/vision, and an annual learning budget. We are an equal-opportunity employer.
