Department of Marine Biology — Coastal Research Group

Research Themes & active projects

Coastal ecosystems under thermal stress

Our group studies how warming and acidification reshape kelp forests and seagrass meadows along the temperate Pacific coast. Current projects combine long-term subtidal monitoring with controlled mesocosm experiments to forecast tipping points.
