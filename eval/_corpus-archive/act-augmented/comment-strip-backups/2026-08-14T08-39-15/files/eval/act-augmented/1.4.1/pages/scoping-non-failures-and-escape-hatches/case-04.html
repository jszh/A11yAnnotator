Cookie & tracking policy

Aldermere District Council · Last reviewed 12 May 2026

Aldermere District Council uses a small number of cookies to keep this site working and to understand how residents use our online services. This page explains what we store, why, and how you can change your choices at any time.

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for core functions such as keeping you signed in to the resident account portal and remembering items in your bin-collection calendar. They cannot be switched off because the service would not work without them.

Analytics cookies

With your consent we use a self-hosted analytics tool to count page visits. The data is aggregated and never sold. You can review the full list of measurements in our measurement schedule or withdraw consent through the cookie preferences centre at any time.

Contacting us

If you have questions about how the council handles your data, write to the Data Protection Officer at County Hall or use the online enquiry form. We aim to reply within ten working days.

Aldermere District Council is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. This notice is available in large print and other formats on request.
