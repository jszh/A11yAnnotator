The slow art of the printed sentence

By Helena Ostrowski

In an age of infinite scroll, the typeset pull-quote endures because it asks the eye to stop. Our newsroom still renders its quote-of-the-day through a small typesetting service that bakes each line into a branded card.

The discipline of the form — one idea, weighed and set — is, perhaps, the last good argument for the column inch. Read slowly; it was set that way on purpose.
