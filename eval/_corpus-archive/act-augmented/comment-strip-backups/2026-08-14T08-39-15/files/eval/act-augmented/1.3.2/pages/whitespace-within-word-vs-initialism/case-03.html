Why automated checkers pass this page: every input has a programmatically associated, non-empty <label> (correct for/id), so axe/WAVE/Lighthouse report a valid accessible name for all three fields and raise no error. They cannot tell that the third label, P A S S W O R D, is the single word "Password" broken by non-breaking spaces (F32) while SSN and PIN are initialisms the same visual tracking is fine for. Whether a spaced label is a real word being shattered or a legitimate acronym is a human linguistic judgement.
