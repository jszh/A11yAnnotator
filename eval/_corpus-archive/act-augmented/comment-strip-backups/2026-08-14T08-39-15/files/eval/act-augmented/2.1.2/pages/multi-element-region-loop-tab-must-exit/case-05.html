Pour-over vs. French press: which suits your morning?

By Mara Oyelaran · 6 min read

Two of the most popular manual brewing methods produce strikingly different cups. A pour-over accentuates clarity and bright acidity; a French press gives you body and a heavier mouthfeel. Below we break down grind size, water temperature, and timing for each.

If you are short on counter space, the French press wins on simplicity. If you chase single-origin nuance, the pour-over rewards patience.
