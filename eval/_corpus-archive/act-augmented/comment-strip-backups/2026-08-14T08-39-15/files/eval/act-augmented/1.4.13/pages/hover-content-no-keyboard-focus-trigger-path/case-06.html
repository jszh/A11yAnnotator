7. Data retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Records tied to an active policy are retained for the life of the policy. After your relationship with us ends, most records are deleted, but certain categories are held under a Legal hold. A suspension of routine deletion required when records may be relevant to litigation, an audit, or a regulator's request. Held records are preserved unchanged — and beyond our normal retention windows — until the matter formally closes, even if you have asked us to delete your data. for as long as the law requires.

Where retention is governed by statute, the applicable period takes precedence over the defaults above. You may request a copy of the categories we retain at any time.
