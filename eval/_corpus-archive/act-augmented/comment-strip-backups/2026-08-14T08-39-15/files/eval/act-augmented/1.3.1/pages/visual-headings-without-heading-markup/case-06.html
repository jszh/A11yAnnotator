Tidewater Coffee Roasters

Slow coffee, made by hand.

A small-batch roastery on the harbor since 2014.

Where we started

Tidewater began as a single drum roaster in a rented garage two blocks from the water. We sold bags at the Saturday market and learned the craft one scorched batch at a time. Within a year we had a tiny storefront and a waitlist for the house espresso blend.

"The best cup I've had in this city." — Harborfront Review

That review changed everything. The line out the door taught us to scale carefully: better green sourcing, a bigger roaster, and a training program so every barista pulls a consistent shot.

How we roast

We roast to order in 12 kg batches, profiling each origin to highlight its character rather than chasing one house taste. Single origins are roasted lighter to keep their florals; our espresso blends go a touch darker for body and crema.
