Renew your driver license online

Most Marisol drivers can renew for 8 years without visiting an office. It takes about 10 minutes.

You can renew online if your license expires within 12 months, you are under 70, your address is current, and you do not need a REAL ID upgrade.

What you'll need

  1. Your current license number and date of birth.
  2. A credit or debit card for the $42 renewal fee.
  3. Confirmation that your eyesight statement is on file (renewed within 5 years).

How it works

  1. Verify your identity with your license number and the last 4 digits of your SSN.
  2. Confirm or update your mailing address.
  3. Pay the renewal fee. Your new license arrives by mail in 7–10 business days.
