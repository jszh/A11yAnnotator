Webhooks

Webhooks let Pulsegate notify your application when events happen in your account, such as a payment settling or a subscription lapsing. Instead of polling the Events API, you register an HTTPS endpoint and we POST a signed JSON payload to it as each event occurs.

Registering an endpoint

Create a webhook endpoint from the dashboard or via the API. Each endpoint can subscribe to a subset of event types.

POST /v1/webhook_endpoints
{
  "url": "https://example.com/pulsegate/hooks",
  "events": ["payment.settled", "subscription.lapsed"]
}

Verifying signatures

Every delivery includes a Pulsegate-Signature header. Compute the HMAC-SHA256 of the raw request body using your endpoint's signing secret and compare it in constant time before trusting the payload.

Retries & backoff

If your endpoint does not return a 2xx within 10 seconds, we retry with exponential backoff for up to 24 hours. Use the delivery's id to deduplicate, since a successful event may be delivered more than once.
