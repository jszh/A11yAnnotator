Instructions for Form SPK-12: Self-Employment Health Insurance Deduction

Tax year 2025 · Revised January 2026 · OMB No. 1545-0074

Who must file: Complete Form SPK-12 if you were self-employed and paid premiums for medical, dental, or qualified long-term care insurance for yourself, your spouse, or your dependents during the tax year.

Purpose of this form

Form SPK-12 is used to calculate the self-employment health insurance deduction you may claim as an adjustment to income. The deduction cannot exceed your net profit from the trade or business under which the insurance plan is established.

Line-by-line instructions

  1. Line 1. Enter the total amount you paid in the tax year for health, dental, and qualified long-term care insurance.
  2. Line 2. Enter your net profit from the business under which the plan was established, from Schedule C, line 31.
  3. Line 3. Enter the smaller of Line 1 or Line 2. This is generally your allowable deduction.
  4. Line 4. Carry the Line 3 amount to Schedule 1, line 17.

Long-term care premium limits

The amount of qualified long-term care premiums you may include on Line 1 is limited by age at the end of the tax year:
