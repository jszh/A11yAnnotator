Dr. Amara Singh

Partner — Intellectual Property & Patents

Member of the firm since 2011 · Based in the Boston office

a.singh@harborquinn.example  ·  +1 (617) 555–0142

Amara leads the firm’s patent-prosecution practice, with a focus on biotech and medical-device portfolios. Before joining Harbor & Quinn she spent six years as in-house counsel at a genomics startup and holds a Ph.D. in molecular biology, which she puts to daily use translating between inventors and examiners.

She has argued before the Patent Trial and Appeal Board and is a frequent speaker on AI-assisted prior-art search. She is admitted to practice in Massachusetts and before the USPTO.
