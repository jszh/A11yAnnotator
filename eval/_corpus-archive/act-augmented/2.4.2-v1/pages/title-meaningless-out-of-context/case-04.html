Eleanor Margaret Whitfield

March 4, 1939 — June 11, 2026

Eleanor Margaret Whitfield, beloved schoolteacher of Asheville for thirty-eight years, passed away peacefully at home surrounded by her family. She is survived by her three children, seven grandchildren, and her sister Ruth Donnelly.

Eleanor taught fourth grade at Vance Elementary, where generations of students remember her kindness and her insistence that everyone learn the names of the birds in the schoolyard. She loved gardening, choral singing, and Sunday crosswords.
