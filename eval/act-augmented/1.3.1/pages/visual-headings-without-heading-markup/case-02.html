Returns & Refunds

Everything you need to know about sending an item back, exchanges, and how refunds are processed.

How long do I have to return an item?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Custom-engraved and final-sale pieces are excluded; these are marked on the product page before purchase.

Do I have to pay for return shipping?

Returns within the contiguous US are free — print the prepaid label from your order page. For international orders, return shipping is deducted from the refund unless the item arrived damaged.

When will I get my money back?

Once our warehouse scans your return, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Your bank may take an additional cycle to post the credit, so allow up to ten days to see it on your statement.

Can I exchange instead of refund?

Yes. Start a return and choose "Exchange" to swap size or color at no extra cost, subject to availability. If the new item costs more, we'll charge the difference.

What if my item arrived damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll send a replacement or full refund right away — no need to ship the damaged item back.
