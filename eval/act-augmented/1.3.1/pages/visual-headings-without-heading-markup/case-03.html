Open Data Policy

Principles and procedures for the publication and reuse of institutional research data

1. Scope

This policy applies to all research data generated by staff, students, and affiliated researchers of the Institute, regardless of funding source, where the data are not subject to a binding confidentiality or commercial-licensing obligation.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this policy, the following terms apply.

2.1 Research data

Recorded factual material commonly accepted in the research community as necessary to validate findings, including measurements, survey responses, and code.

2.2 Open access

Free, immediate, online availability under a licence that permits reuse with attribution.

3. Data-Sharing Principles

The Institute adopts the principle that data should be "as open as possible, as closed as necessary." Researchers are expected to:

a) deposit datasets in an approved repository at the point of publication;

b) assign a persistent identifier (DOI) to each released dataset;

c) apply the least-restrictive licence consistent with any legal constraints.

4. Exceptions and Embargoes

Where data contain personal information, are subject to a third-party agreement, or have foreseeable commercial value, release may be delayed or restricted. Requests for an embargo of more than twelve months require approval from the Research Governance Committee.

5. Review

This policy is reviewed every two years. The next scheduled review is March 2028. Questions should be directed to the Office of Research Integrity.
