Brown-Butter Banana Bread

By Dilan Owusu · Updated June 2026

Prep 15 min Bake 55 min Makes 1 loaf

Browning the butter first gives this loaf a deep, nutty caramel note that plain melted butter can't touch. Use very ripe, freckled bananas for the best flavor and a moist crumb.

Ingredients

  • 113 g (½ cup) unsalted butter
  • 3 large very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1½ cups)
  • 150 g (¾ cup) light brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 190 g (1½ cups) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda · ½ tsp fine salt · ½ tsp cinnamon
Method
  1. Melt the butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling, until it smells nutty and the milk solids turn golden-brown, 5–7 minutes. Let cool slightly.
  2. Whisk the browned butter with the mashed bananas, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon. Fold the dry into the wet just until no streaks remain — do not overmix.
  4. Scrape into a parchment-lined 9×5 loaf pan and bake at 175°C (350°F) for 50–58 minutes, until a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  5. Cool in the pan 15 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.
Notes & Substitutions

No ripe bananas? Roast unpeeled bananas at 150°C for 15 minutes to soften and sweeten them.

For a dairy-free loaf, brown a plant butter the same way — the flavor is milder but works.

Wrap cooled slices and freeze up to 3 months; toast straight from frozen.
