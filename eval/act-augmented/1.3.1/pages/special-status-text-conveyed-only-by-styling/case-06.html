Replacing the Filter Cartridge

You will need the replacement cartridge (model HX-3F) and a towel. The whole job takes about ten minutes.

  1. Locate the blue inlet valve on the cold-water line beneath the sink.
  2. Important: turn the blue inlet valve fully clockwise to shut off the water supply before continuing. If the valve is left open, water will spray from the housing when it is opened in the next step.
  3. Place the towel under the filter housing to catch residual water.
  4. Twist the filter housing a quarter-turn counter-clockwise and pull it straight down to remove it.
  5. Lift out the spent cartridge and discard it. Insert the new HX-3F cartridge, ribbed end up, until it seats with a soft click.
  6. Refit the housing: align the arrows, push up, and twist a quarter-turn clockwise until snug.
  7. Turn the blue inlet valve fully counter-clockwise to restore the water supply, then check the housing seam for drips for one minute.

Replace the cartridge every six months, or sooner if water flow slows noticeably.
