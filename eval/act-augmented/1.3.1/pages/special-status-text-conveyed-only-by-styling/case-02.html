Amendment 2026-04 to the Tenant Noise By-law

By-law No. 118 · Tabled for council reading on 5 April 2026

This page shows the by-law as it will read once Amendment 2026-04 takes effect. Edits are marked in the usual way.

Section 4 — Quiet hours

  1. 4.1 No tenant shall create noise audible beyond their unit between the hours of 10:00 p.m. 9:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. on any day.
  2. 4.2 The restriction in 4.1 applies on weekdays only. all days, including weekends and statutory holidays.
  3. 4.3 Amplified music played on a balcony, patio, or other outdoor portion of a unit is prohibited at all hours.

Section 6 — Enforcement

  1. 6.1 A first contravention shall result in a written warning.
  2. 6.2 A second contravention within twelve months shall result in a fine of $150. $300.
  3. 6.3 No fine shall be issued unless a noise-measurement reading has been taken by a by-law officer using calibrated equipment.

All other provisions of By-law No. 118 remain in force as previously enacted.
