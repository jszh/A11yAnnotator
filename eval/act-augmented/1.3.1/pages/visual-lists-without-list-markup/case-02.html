For severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis). Follow the steps IN ORDER. A wrong order can deliver the dose into your thumb.

AllerStop™ 0.3 mg Auto-Injector — How to Use

Keep this card with the injector. Read it before an emergency happens.

Administration

  • 1. Remove the injector from the clear carrier tube.
  • 2. Form a fist around the unit so the orange tip points down. Never put your thumb over either end.
  • 3. Pull off the blue safety release straight up. Do not bend or twist it.
  • 4. Swing and push the orange tip firmly into the middle of the outer thigh until it clicks.
  • 5. Hold in place for 3 seconds, then remove and massage the area for 10 seconds.
  • 6. Call emergency services immediately and tell them epinephrine was given.

If symptoms return, a second dose may be given after 5 minutes only on medical advice.
