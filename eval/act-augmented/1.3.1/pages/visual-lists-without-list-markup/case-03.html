Glossary of Cupping Terms

A quick reference for tasting vocabulary used on our cupping forms. Bring this to your first calibration session.

Body

The tactile weight or thickness of the coffee on the palate, from tea-like to syrupy.

Crema

The reddish-brown foam of emulsified oils that sits atop a freshly pulled espresso shot.

Quaker

An underripe bean that fails to brown during roasting, producing a papery, peanut-like off-flavor.

Terroir

The combined effect of soil, altitude, and climate on a coffee's intrinsic flavor character.

Washed Process

A method in which fruit is removed before drying, typically yielding a cleaner, brighter cup.

Defect Score

Points deducted on the SCA form for taints and faults found in the sample.

Questions about a term? Ask your lead roaster before scoring.
