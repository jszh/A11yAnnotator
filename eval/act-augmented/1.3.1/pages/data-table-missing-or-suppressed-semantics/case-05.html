Emergency first aid

How to use an adrenaline auto-injector (EpiPen)

For someone having a severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis). Follow these steps in order.

Call emergency services first. An auto-injector buys time; it is not a substitute for emergency medical care.

Steps

  1. Remove the auto-injector from its tube and hold it in your dominant hand with your thumb closest to the coloured safety cap.
  2. Pull off the blue safety cap. Do not put your thumb, fingers, or hand over the orange tip at any point.
  3. Place the orange tip against the outer mid-thigh, at a right angle to the leg. It can be given through clothing if needed.
  4. Push down hard until you hear a click, then hold firmly in place for 3 seconds (count slowly: one…two…three).
  5. Remove the injector and massage the area for 10 seconds. Note the time the dose was given.
  6. If symptoms do not improve in 5 minutes, give a second auto-injector into the other thigh.

Adapted from national resuscitation council guidance. Last reviewed January.
