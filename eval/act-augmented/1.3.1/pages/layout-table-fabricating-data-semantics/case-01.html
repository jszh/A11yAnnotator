Order a Certified Copy of a Vital Record

Certified copies of birth, death, and marriage records for events that occurred in Northbridge County may be ordered online, by mail, or in person at the County Clerk's office. Online orders are processed within five to seven business days. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for all certified-copy requests.

Begin a request by entering the registrant's full legal name and the approximate date of the event. If you are unsure of the exact date, a five-year search window is included at no additional charge.

Office Hours

Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
Closed county holidays.

Fees

Certified copy: $24 each.
Additional copies (same order): $8 each.

The Clerk's office will close at noon on November 27 for staff training.
