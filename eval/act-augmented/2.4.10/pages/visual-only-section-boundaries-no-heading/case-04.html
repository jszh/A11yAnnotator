From the trail crew Summer ridges are finally open

The last of the Cascade snowpack cleared this week, and the high routes above Snow Lake are walkable again. This issue rounds up trip reports, a gear pick, a member story, and the July outing calendar.
Trail report Granite Mountain via the south spur

9.1 miles round trip, 3,800 ft gain. The new switchbacks below the lookout are holding up well. Water is reliable until the 4,000-ft bench; carry two liters past that. Bugs are moderate — bring a head net for the meadow.
Gear pick of the month A 1.9 oz wind shell that earns its place

We tested four ultralight wind shells on exposed ridgelines. The Zephyr Pro packs to the size of a lime, blocks a 25 mph gust, and breathes on the climb. At $99 it is not cheap, but it replaced two heavier layers in our kits.
Member spotlight Priya finished the Issaquah Alps traverse

Club member Priya Nair linked Squak, Tiger, and Cougar in a single 28-mile push to raise funds for trail maintenance. "The crews who build these paths rarely get thanked," she said. Donations are still open through the end of June.
