Slow-Braised Short Rib Ragu

Serves 4 · Active 30 min · Total 3 hours

Before you start

This is a low-and-slow Sunday braise that mostly looks after itself once it is in the oven. Read the whole method before you begin so the timings line up.

You will need a heavy lidded casserole that goes from hob to oven. Pull the ribs out of the fridge an hour ahead so they come up to room temperature.

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg bone-in beef short ribs
  • 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks and 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée, 250 ml red wine, 400 ml beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves, a sprig of thyme, salt and pepper

  1. Heat a little oil and sear the seasoned ribs on all sides until deeply browned, then set them aside.
  2. Soften the diced carrot, celery and onion in the same pan; stir in the tomato purée and cook for a minute.
  3. Pour in the wine to deglaze, return the ribs, add the stock and herbs, then cover and braise in a low oven for two and a half hours until the meat falls from the bone.

To serve

Lift out the ribs, shred the meat and discard the bones. Skim the sauce, fold the meat back through it, and toss with cooked pappardelle. Finish with grated parmesan.
