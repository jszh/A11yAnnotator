Slow-roast harissa carrots with whipped feta

Charred at the edges, soft in the middle, with a rose-harissa glaze and a cool, lemony feta to spoon underneath.

Serves 4 as a side · 45 minutes · Vegetarian

About this dish

Roasting carrots whole-ish and slow lets their sugars caramelise without going to mush. The harissa is brushed on in the last ten minutes so it glazes rather than burns. If your carrots are thick, halve them lengthways so they cook evenly.

Ingredients

  1. 600 g carrots, scrubbed, large ones halved lengthways
  2. 2 tbsp rose harissa
  3. 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to finish
  4. 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  5. 150 g feta, 3 tbsp Greek yoghurt, zest of half a lemon
  6. A handful of coriander and toasted pumpkin seeds, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Toss the carrots with the oil and a good pinch of salt and roast for 25 minutes.
  2. Stir the harissa with the honey. Brush over the carrots and roast another 10–12 minutes until sticky and tender.
  3. Blitz the feta, yoghurt and lemon zest until whipped. Spread on a plate, pile the carrots on top, and finish with coriander, seeds and a thread of oil.
