Apply for a Residential Parking Permit

Residents of permit zones A through F may apply online for an annual on-street parking permit. Read each part of this page before you begin — your application will be rejected if your vehicle or address is not eligible.

Section Heading

You are eligible only if your primary residence is inside one of the lettered permit zones (A–F) and the vehicle is registered to that address. Properties on the boundary streets listed in Schedule 2 of the Parking Bylaw are not part of any zone and do not qualify.

  • The vehicle registration address must match your proof-of-residency document.
  • Each household may hold a maximum of two residential permits at one time.
  • Commercial vehicles over 3.5 tonnes are excluded regardless of address.

Section Heading

Permit fees are charged annually and are not pro-rated. Payment is taken at the end of the application; permits are not issued until payment clears.

Annual residential permit fees by vehicle count
PermitAnnual feeRenewal window
First vehicle$45Within 30 days of expiry
Second vehicle$110Within 30 days of expiry
Replacement (lost/stolen)$20Any time

Fees are non-refundable once a permit is printed, even if you move out of the zone during the permit year.

Section Heading

Have your documents ready before you start; the online form times out after twenty minutes of inactivity and does not save partial progress.

  1. Scan your vehicle registration and a recent utility bill (issued within 90 days).
  2. Create or sign in to your Harwick.gov account.
  3. Enter your plate number and address; the system checks your zone automatically.
  4. Pay the applicable fee and download your temporary permit receipt.
