Renew a vehicle registration

You can renew most passenger-vehicle registrations online up to 90 days before they expire. You'll need your license-plate number and the renewal notice mailed to the registered owner. Renewals are processed within two business days and a new sticker is mailed to the address on file.

Before you start: emissions testing must be current for vehicles registered in the Clearwater air-quality zone. If your test is expired, the system will not let you complete the renewal.
  1. Enter your plate number and the last four digits of the VIN.
  2. Confirm the registered owner and mailing address.
  3. Review fees, including any late penalty if the registration has lapsed.
  4. Pay by card or e-check and download your receipt.
