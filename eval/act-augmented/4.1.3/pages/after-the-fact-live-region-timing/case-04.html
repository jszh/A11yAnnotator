Why automated tools cannot catch this: When a price-alert fires, a custom element <price-alert> is created and mounted; in its connectedCallback it builds a child <div role="status" aria-live="assertive"> whose textContent is set BEFORE that child (and the whole element) is in the document. Inspect the page afterward and you find a valid live region with the alert text — axe/WAVE/Lighthouse pass it. But the live region and its message were assembled inside connectedCallback as the element entered the DOM, so the region was never present and empty beforehand and the alert is not announced. No DOM snapshot reveals that the region's content existed at the instant it was connected.
