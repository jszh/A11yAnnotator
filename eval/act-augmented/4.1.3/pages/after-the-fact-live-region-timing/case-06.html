Why this PASSES (and why it is the boundary control): The snackbar <div id="snackbar" role="status" aria-live="polite"> is present in the markup at page load as an empty, registered live region. Adding to cart writes ONLY textContent into that pre-existing region (“Yirgacheffe added to your cart. 1 item.”) and toggles a CSS class to slide it in. Because the region existed and was empty when the AT registered it, the later text mutation IS announced. This is the correct ARIA19/ARIA22 step-1 pattern and the exact opposite of the born-with-content failures in case-01 through case-05 — same final markup, but the region pre-existed the message. The marker data-region-preexisting-empty="true" records the correct timing.
