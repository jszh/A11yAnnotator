Exporting a report

Cartograph can export any saved report to PDF, CSV, or a static HTML bundle. This guide walks through the PDF path, which is the most common for sharing with stakeholders.

Open the Export dialog

  1. Open the report you want to export from the Reports sidebar.
  2. Choose File › Export… from the menu bar, or press Ctrl/Cmd + E.
  3. In the dialog, pick a Format and decide whether to include metadata.
Tip: Leave Include metadata unchecked for external sharing so editor names and internal notes are stripped from the PDF.

Finish the export

  1. Click Export and choose a destination folder.
  2. Cartograph renders the PDF and reveals it in your file browser when done.
