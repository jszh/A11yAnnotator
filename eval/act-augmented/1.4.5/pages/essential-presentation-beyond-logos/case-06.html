Mountain High, Water Long

Seal script (篆書) · ink on silk · c. 1080, Northern Song dynasty · Object 1992.114

Transcription (modern Unicode)

山高水長

shān gāo shuǐ cháng

Translation

"The mountain is high, the water runs long" — a classical expression of enduring virtue and lasting reputation, often presented as a gift of respect.

About this work

The image above reproduces the original scroll. Its value to scholars lies in the brushwork itself — the stroke formation and ink character of this particular hand — which a modern font cannot reproduce. For readers who need adjustable text, the characters, their pronunciation, and an English translation are provided above as selectable, resizable text.
