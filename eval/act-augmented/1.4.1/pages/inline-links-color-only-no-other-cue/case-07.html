1. Scope

1.1 This policy governs your use of Northgate online and mobile banking. It supplements, and should be read together with, the general terms and conditions. Where this policy conflicts with those terms, the general terms prevail.

2.1 You must not use the service to commit fraud, to access another customer’s account without authority, or to circumvent the security controls described in our information security policy. Doing so may result in suspension and referral to the relevant authorities.

3.1 We may amend this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified at least 30 days in advance in line with our change-notification procedure.
