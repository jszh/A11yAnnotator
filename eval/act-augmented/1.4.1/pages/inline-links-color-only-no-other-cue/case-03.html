Sourdough focaccia, three ways

Tested 22 times · Hands-on 30 min · Total 18 hr · Makes one half-sheet

This is the dough I keep coming back to. It is forgiving, it loves a long cold proof, and it takes toppings beautifully. If you have never built a starter, read my seven-day starter guide first — everything below assumes a lively, recently-fed culture. Already comfortable? Jump to the shaping notes or the topping ideas.

For the dough

  • 500 g bread flour (I use the high-protein blend)
  • 400 g water, room temperature
  • 100 g active starter
  • 12 g fine sea salt
  • 40 g extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the pan

Method

Mix the flour and water and let it rest for an hour — this autolyse does a lot of the kneading for you, as I explain in the autolyse explainer. Add the starter and salt, fold every thirty minutes for two hours, then move the covered bowl to the fridge overnight. The next day, stretch into an oiled pan, dimple generously, and bake hot. For the crackliest crust, see the steam & heat tips.
