We plant the forests no one else will

Verdant Trust restores degraded upland to native broadleaf woodland across the British Isles. Since 2011 our volunteers have planted 2.4 million trees on land written off as unrecoverable.

Ninety-one pence of every pound goes directly into the ground. The rest keeps the nurseries running and the maps honest.
