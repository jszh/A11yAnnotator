Central bank holds rates as inflation cools to 2.4%

By Priya Raman · 06:30 GMT

Policymakers left the benchmark rate unchanged for a third consecutive meeting, signalling that the tightening cycle is likely complete even as services prices remain sticky.

Markets had priced in a hold with near certainty; attention now turns to the projected path of cuts in the second half of the year. The pound was little changed against the dollar.

“The committee will move carefully,” the governor said, declining to commit to a timetable. Bond yields slipped at the short end as traders nudged forward their expectations for the first reduction.
