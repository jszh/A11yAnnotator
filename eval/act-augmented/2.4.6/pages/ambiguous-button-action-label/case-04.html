Form RT-1: Annual Resident Tax Return

Filing deadline for tax year 2025 is April 15, 2026. Late returns accrue interest from the deadline.

Form RT-1 is the standard return for individuals who were residents of Cedar County for any part of the 2025 tax year. The fillable PDF includes the income, deduction, and credit schedules. Most residents can complete it in under an hour with their W-2 and 1099 forms to hand.

Prefer paper? Form RT-1 is also available at any Cedar County library branch and at the Revenue Service Center, 200 Birchwood Ave.
