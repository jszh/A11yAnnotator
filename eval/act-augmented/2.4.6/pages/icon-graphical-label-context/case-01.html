Acme Logistics, Inc.

Quarterly Report — Three months ended 30 September 2025

Net revenue for the quarter rose 8.4% year over year to $214.7 million, driven by sustained demand in the cross-border freight segment and the full-quarter contribution of the Rotterdam consolidation hub acquired in May. Operating margin expanded 110 basis points as the prior-year integration charges did not recur.

Cash and cash equivalents stood at $61.2 million at quarter end. The company repaid $18.0 million of the revolving facility and ended the period with no outstanding commercial-paper balances. Management reaffirms full-year guidance.
