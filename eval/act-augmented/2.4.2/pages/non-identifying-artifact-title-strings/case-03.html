Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective 1 March 2024

Unworn items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

How to start a return

Sign in to your account, open Order History, choose the order, and select Start a return. You'll receive a prepaid shipping label by email within one business day.

Refund timing

Once our warehouse receives and inspects your return, refunds are issued within 5–7 business days. Your bank may take additional time to post the credit.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay return shipping?
No. Domestic returns include a prepaid label at no cost to you.
Can I exchange instead of refund?
Yes — choose Exchange in the return flow and select the new size or colour.
What about final-sale items?
Items marked “Final sale” on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged.
