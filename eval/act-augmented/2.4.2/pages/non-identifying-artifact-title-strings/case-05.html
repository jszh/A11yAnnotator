Dr. Lena Okafor

Keynote Speaker · Director of AI Safety, Meridian Labs

Keynote: Calibrating Trust in Autonomous Decision Systems

Thursday 09:30 – 10:15 · Main Auditorium

How do we know when to trust a model's recommendation? Dr. Okafor presents a practical framework for measuring and communicating model uncertainty to the people who must act on it.

About Dr. Okafor

Lena Okafor leads the AI Safety group at Meridian Labs, where her team studies calibration, robustness, and human oversight of deployed models. She previously spent eight years in clinical-decision-support research and holds a PhD in statistics from the University of Edinburgh.

Speaking topics

  • Uncertainty quantification for high-stakes predictions
  • Designing human-in-the-loop oversight that people actually use
  • Communicating model confidence to non-technical stakeholders
