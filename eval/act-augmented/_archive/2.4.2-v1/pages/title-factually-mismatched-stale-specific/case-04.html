Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: January 12, 2026

This policy explains how Maple & Oak Home ships orders, our delivery timeframes, and shipping costs. For information about sending an item back, see our separate Returns & Refunds Policy.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking once your order leaves our warehouse.

Tracking your shipment

Once shipped, track your delivery from the link in your confirmation email or in your account under "Order history." Shipping carriers update tracking within 24 hours of pickup.

Shipping restrictions

We currently ship within the contiguous United States only. We do not ship to P.O. boxes for freight items. International shipping is not available at this time.

Undeliverable shipments

If a shipment is returned to us as undeliverable, our team will email you to arrange re-delivery; additional shipping charges may apply.
