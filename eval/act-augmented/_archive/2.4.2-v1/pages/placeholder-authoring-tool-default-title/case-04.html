Lemon & Thyme Roast Chicken

A one-pan Sunday roast with crisp skin and a bright pan sauce

Serves: 4 Prep: 20 min Cook: 1 hr 15 min Oven: 200 °C / 400 °F
[ Photo: golden lemon-and-thyme roast chicken in a cast-iron pan ]

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 1.6 kg
  • 2 unwaxed lemons, one halved, one sliced
  • 1 small bunch fresh thyme
  • 4 cloves garlic, lightly crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 40 g butter, softened
  • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 150 ml dry white wine or chicken stock

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Pat the chicken dry and rub all over with the softened butter, then season generously inside and out.
  2. Stuff the cavity with the halved lemon, the garlic cloves, and half the thyme.
  3. Sit the chicken in a roasting pan, scatter the lemon slices and remaining thyme around it, and drizzle with the olive oil.
  4. Roast for 1 hour 15 minutes, basting once or twice, until the juices run clear and the thickest part of the thigh reaches 74 °C.
  5. Lift the chicken onto a board to rest for 15 minutes. Set the pan over medium heat, pour in the wine, and scrape up the sticky bits to make a quick pan sauce.
  6. Carve and serve with the lemony pan sauce spooned over.

Tip: For extra-crisp skin, leave the seasoned chicken uncovered in the fridge for a few hours before roasting to dry the skin.
