About the X9

The X9 is Acme Audio's flagship over-ear headphone. Its adaptive noise-cancelling engine samples ambient sound 50,000 times per second and adjusts the cancellation profile in real time, so the cabin roar of a flight and the hum of an open-plan office are both suppressed without the "pressure" sensation common to older ANC sets.

A new 40 mm bio-cellulose driver delivers a flatter midrange than the X8, and the memory-foam ear cushions are wrapped in protein leather rated for 500 hours of contact wear. Fast charging gives 5 hours of playback from a 10-minute charge over USB-C.
