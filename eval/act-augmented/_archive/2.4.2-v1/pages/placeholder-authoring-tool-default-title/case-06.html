How to reset your Nimbus Mail password

Last updated 2 weeks ago · 3 min read

If you have forgotten your password or think your account may have been compromised, you can reset it from the sign-in screen in a few steps. You will need access to the email address or phone number on your account.

Reset from the sign-in screen

  1. Go to the Nimbus Mail sign-in page and select Forgot password? below the password field.
  2. Enter the email address associated with your account and select Continue.
  3. Choose how you want to receive your verification code — by email or by text message — and select Send code.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code within 10 minutes, then select Verify.
  5. Create a new password that is at least 12 characters long and has not been used on your account before, then select Save.
Note: For your security, all other signed-in sessions are ended automatically when you reset your password. You will need to sign in again on your other devices.

Didn't get a code?

Check your spam folder, confirm the email address is spelled correctly, and wait a full minute before requesting a new code. If you no longer have access to your recovery email or phone, use the Account recovery form instead.
