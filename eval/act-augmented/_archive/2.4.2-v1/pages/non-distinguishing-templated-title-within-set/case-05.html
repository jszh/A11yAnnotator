How do I request a refund for a charge I didn’t recognize?

If you see a Brightwave Mobile charge you don’t recognize, you can request a refund directly from the billing screen within 60 days of the charge date. Most refunds are reviewed within 3–5 business days.

Steps

  1. Open the Brightwave app and go to Account › Billing history.
  2. Tap the charge in question to open its detail view.
  3. Choose Dispute this charge and pick a reason.
  4. Submit. You’ll get a confirmation email with a case number.
Note: Charges older than 60 days must be disputed through your bank, not through the Help Center.
