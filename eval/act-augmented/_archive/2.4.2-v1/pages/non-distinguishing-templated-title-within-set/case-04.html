checkout-service #4821

Payment retry loops indefinitely when the gateway returns HTTP 402

Open Bug Priority: High

Description

When the payment gateway returns 402 Payment Required, the retry handler treats it as a transient error and re-submits the same charge forever. We saw 11,400 duplicate attempts on order ORD-99214 before the circuit breaker tripped.

Steps to reproduce: submit an order with a card that the sandbox flags as "insufficient funds", watch retryCharge() spin. Expected: fail fast after 3 attempts and surface the decline to the user.
