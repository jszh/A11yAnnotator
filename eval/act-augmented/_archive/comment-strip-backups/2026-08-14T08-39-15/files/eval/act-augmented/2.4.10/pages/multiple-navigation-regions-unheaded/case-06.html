City Hall approves $42M transit budget after marathon hearing

By Priya Anand · Local Government · 6 min read

The City Council voted 6–3 late Friday to adopt a $42 million transit budget that restores weekend service on three suburban routes and funds a pilot of all-electric shuttles downtown. The vote followed nearly five hours of public comment.

What changed in the final vote

An amendment introduced by Councilmember Reyes shifted $1.8 million from a parking-garage study to weekend bus service, a move transit advocates had pressed for since March.

What riders can expect

Weekend service on Routes 12, 19, and 27 is scheduled to resume in September. The electric-shuttle pilot will begin with four vehicles on a single downtown loop.
