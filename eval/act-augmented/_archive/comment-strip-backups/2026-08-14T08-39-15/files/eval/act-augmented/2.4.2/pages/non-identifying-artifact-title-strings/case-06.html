Account Security

Set up two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second verification step when you sign in, so your account stays protected even if your password is exposed. Follow the steps below to enable it with an authenticator app.

Before you begin

Install an authenticator app such as a TOTP-compatible app on your phone. You'll scan a QR code during setup.

Enable 2FA

  1. Open Settings › Security and select Two-factor authentication.
  2. Choose Authenticator app and select Set up.
  3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code your app shows to confirm, then select Turn on.
Save your recovery codes. After enabling 2FA, download the recovery codes and store them somewhere safe. You'll need one if you lose access to your phone.

Turn off 2FA

Return to Settings › Security and select Turn off next to two-factor authentication. You'll be asked to confirm with your current verification method.
