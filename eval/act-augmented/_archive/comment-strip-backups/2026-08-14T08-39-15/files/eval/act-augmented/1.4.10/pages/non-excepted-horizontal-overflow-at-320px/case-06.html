What to watch

The Mountain region's March spike is almost entirely new accounts rather than expansion of existing ones, which is encouraging for retention but worth a sanity check on discounting. Coastal volume softened in February before recovering, so we should confirm that was the holiday calendar and not a fulfillment issue. Please come Thursday with one hypothesis per region.
