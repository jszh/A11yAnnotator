Skyline Braces As The Storm Front Clears The Bright Western Sky

By Dana Okafor · Photographs from the Riverside Tower

Forecasters spent Thursday watching a wall of cloud break apart over the western suburbs, leaving the downtown towers silhouetted against a hard, colourless glare. The reprieve is temporary: a second band is expected to reach the harbour by midnight, and the city has reopened two emergency shelters along the embankment.

Transit officials warned that the elevated line may run on a reduced schedule through the weekend while crews inspect the spans that carried the brunt of Wednesday's wind.
