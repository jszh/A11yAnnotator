Why automated tools miss this. No control uses outline:none; a focus indicator is present and a single-element focused/unfocused pixel comparison is non-zero (the outline offset shifts by 1 px on focus). axe-core, WAVE and Lighthouse therefore report no 2.4.7 violation. But every field already carries a resting 2px solid #1a73e8 border and outline — the same blue and weight as the focus ring — so when a field focuses, a human sees no distinguishing change and cannot tell which of the seven controls holds focus while tabbing. Only a person comparing the resting blue-bordered look against the focused look can judge that the indicator is present-but-not-perceptible (F78 mode 2).
