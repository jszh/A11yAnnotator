Walking Lisbon's Baixa: a half-day on foot

By Cláudia Reis · 11 min read

Start where the trams turn: the Baixa grid was rebuilt on a clean plan after the 1755 earthquake, so for once a Lisbon walk is mostly flat. Aim for early evening, when the limestone façades catch the light and the pavement mosaics glow.

From the riverfront, the great triumphal arch frames the entrance to the main pedestrian spine. Duck into the side streets for tiled storefronts, then circle back for a pastel de nata before the cafés fill up. Allow three hours if you stop to look — four if you stop to eat.
