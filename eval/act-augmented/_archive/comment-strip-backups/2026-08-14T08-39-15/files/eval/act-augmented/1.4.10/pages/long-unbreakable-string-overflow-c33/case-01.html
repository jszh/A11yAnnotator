Why didn’t my password-reset email arrive, and what does the link inside it look like?

Updated 11 June 2026 · Article #4471 · 3 min read

If you requested a password reset and the email hasn’t shown up, first check your spam or junk folder, then confirm you typed the same address you registered with. Reset messages can take up to ten minutes to be delivered during busy periods, so please wait a little while before requesting a second one — each new request invalidates the link in the previous email.

Some customers tell us the message did arrive but the button looked broken, so they were worried it was a phishing attempt. To reassure you, here is exactly what the safe, genuine reset link from no-reply@northwind.example looks like. The address always begins with our domain and ends with a one-time token that is unique to your request:

The genuine reset link

Click the button in your email, or if your client stripped the button, copy and paste this exact address into your browser’s address bar:

https://account.northwind.example/auth/reset?campaign=pwd-reset-2026&rid=u9183&token=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9.aZ7Qk2Lm4Vx9Tn0Pr5Yd8Wg3Bc6Hf1Js4Ke7Lp2Mq5Nt8Ov1Rw4Ux7Zy0Ab3Cd6Ef9Gh2Ij5Kl8Mn1Op4Qz9Wp2

If the address you received does not start with account.northwind.example, do not click it — report it to us instead.

Still stuck? Our support team can send a fresh link to your inbox while you are on the line. We never ask for your password, and we will never read the token back to you over the phone.
