Brown Butter Miso Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep 20 minChill 1 hrBake 11 minMakes 18 cookies

These cookies lean on two umami tricks — nutty browned butter and a spoonful of white miso — to push a classic chocolate chip cookie toward deep, almost-toffee flavor. The dough rests in the fridge so the butter re-solidifies and the cookies bake up thick with crisp edges and a fudgy center.

Ingredients

  • 225 g unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp white (shiro) miso
  • 200 g light brown sugar, packed
  • 100 g granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs, cold
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 310 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 250 g dark chocolate, chopped into chunks
  • — flaky sea salt, for topping

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a light-colored pan over medium heat, swirling, until it foams and the milk solids turn golden-brown and smell nutty, 5–7 minutes. Pour into a heatproof bowl, whisk in the miso, and let cool 15 minutes.
  2. Whisk both sugars into the browned butter until smooth, then beat in the eggs one at a time and the vanilla. Beat 1 minute until glossy.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour and baking soda. Fold into the wet mixture just until no dry streaks remain, then fold in the chocolate.
  4. Cover and chill the dough at least 1 hour (overnight is better). Heat the oven to 180 °C / 350 °F and line two trays with parchment.
  5. Scoop 60 g mounds, space well apart, and bake 11–12 minutes until the edges are set but the centers look underdone. Sprinkle with flaky salt and cool on the tray 10 minutes before moving.
