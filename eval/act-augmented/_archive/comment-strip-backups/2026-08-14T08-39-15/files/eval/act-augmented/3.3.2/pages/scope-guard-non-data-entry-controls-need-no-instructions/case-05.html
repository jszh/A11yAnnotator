Weeknight miso-glazed salmon

Serves 225 minutesby Priya Anand

A sweet-savoury glaze comes together in the time it takes the rice to cook. The whole thing is a Tuesday-night affair: one pan, one bowl, and a grill that does the rest.

Ingredients

Add to shopping list

Scale:
  • skin-on salmon
  • white miso paste
  • mirin
  • garlic, grated
  • short-grain rice
  • scallions & sesame

Method

Whisk the miso, mirin, a tablespoon of soy, and the grated garlic into a smooth paste.
Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook by your usual method while you glaze the fish.
Pat the salmon dry, coat with the glaze, and grill 6–8 minutes until the top caramelises and the fish flakes.
Spoon over rice, shower with sliced scallions and sesame, and serve at once.
