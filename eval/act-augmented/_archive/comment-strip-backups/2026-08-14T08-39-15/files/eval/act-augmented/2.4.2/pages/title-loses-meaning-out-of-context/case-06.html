Installing

Install the Aurora CLI on macOS, Linux, or Windows. The CLI requires no runtime dependencies and ships as a single static binary.

Package managers

brew install aurora-labs/tap/aurora

Verify the installation

Confirm the CLI is on your PATH and prints its version:

aurora --version
# aurora 2.7.1 (darwin/arm64)

Next steps

With the CLI installed, continue to Authentication to connect it to your Aurora workspace.
