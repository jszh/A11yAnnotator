How to read a nutrition label

Understanding the panel on packaged food helps you compare products and manage portions. The diagram below walks through each part of a standard label.

Why serving size comes first

Every other number on the panel is calculated per serving. If a package contains several servings, the calories and nutrients you actually consume scale with how much you eat.

Tip: when two products list different serving sizes, convert to the same amount before you compare calories or sodium.
