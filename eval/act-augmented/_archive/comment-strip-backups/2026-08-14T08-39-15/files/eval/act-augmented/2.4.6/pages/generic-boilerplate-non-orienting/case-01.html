Orders & Returns Help

Everything you need to know once you've placed an order with us.

On this page Information · More Information · Additional Information

Information

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be unworn and in their original packaging with tags attached. To start a return, sign in to your account, open the order, and select Request a return. We email a prepaid label within one business day.

Refunds are issued once the warehouse scans the returned parcel — typically 3–5 business days after it arrives. Sale items marked Final Sale and gift cards cannot be refunded. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

More Information

Standard delivery within the contiguous mainland takes 3–6 business days and is free on orders over £50. Express delivery (1–2 business days) is a flat £7.95. Orders placed after 2 p.m. ship the next business day. We do not deliver on public holidays.

Additional Information

All technical outerwear and footwear carry a two-year manufacturer's warranty against faults in materials and workmanship, starting from the delivery date. The warranty does not cover wear from normal use, accidental damage, or alterations.

To make a warranty claim, email a photo of the fault and your order number to our care team. Approved claims are repaired, replaced, or credited at our discretion. The warranty is in addition to your statutory consumer rights, which are not affected.
