Request a Curbside Recycling Bin

Households within city limits may request one free 65-gallon recycling cart. Allow 7–10 business days for delivery.

Why automated tools cannot catch this: After you press “Submit request,” the final DOM contains a perfectly valid <div role="status">Your recycling cart request was received…</div>. axe-core, WAVE and Lighthouse inspect that resting DOM, see a syntactically correct live region with text, and report no error. They have no signal that the role="status" attribute did not exist as an empty container before the message text was written — the region and its content were created in a single innerHTML operation, so most screen readers never register the region in time to announce it. The defect is purely temporal: it requires reasoning about the ORDER of DOM operations, not the end-state markup.
