The Cartographer's Apprentice — a quietly devastating debut

★★★★☆

Vesna Hale's first novel maps grief the way her protagonist maps coastlines: obsessively, and never quite to scale. It is a slow book that rewards patience.

The middle act sags, but the final forty pages are some of the most assured writing I have read this year. Recommended, with a box of tissues.
