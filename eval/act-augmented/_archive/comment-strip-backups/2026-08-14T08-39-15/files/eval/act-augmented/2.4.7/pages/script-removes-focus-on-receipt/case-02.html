City council approves the harbor rail extension after a decade of delay

By Dana Okafor · Transit Desk · 9 min read

After ten years of stalled votes and three failed referendums, the city council last night approved funding for the long-promised harbor rail extension, a 6.4-mile line that will connect the eastern districts to the central terminal for the first time.

The 7–2 vote followed a marathon public-comment session in which more than forty residents spoke, most in favor of a project that supporters say will cut peak commute times nearly in half for the dockside neighborhoods.
