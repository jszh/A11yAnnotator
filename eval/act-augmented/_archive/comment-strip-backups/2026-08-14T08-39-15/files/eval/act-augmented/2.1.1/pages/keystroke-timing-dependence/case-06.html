Salt flats at dawn: a week on the Uyuni mirror

By Dana Okafor · 11 min read

Before sunrise the salt crust holds the last of the night's rain in a film barely a centimetre deep, and for twenty minutes the horizon doubles. We walked out at 4 a.m. to catch it.

By six the wind picked up and the mirror broke into ripples. We packed the tripods and drove the last kilometre to the old salt-processing huts on the island of Incahuasi.
