Frequently asked questions

A few things our subscribers ask most often.

Details

When am I charged for my subscription?

Your card is charged on the same calendar day each month — the day you first subscribed. If you started on the 31st, you'll be billed on the last day of shorter months. We email a receipt the moment a payment succeeds, and you can update your card under Account → Billing at any time. Annual plans renew once a year and can be switched to monthly before the renewal date.

Can I skip a month or pause my plan?

Yes. From your subscription dashboard you can skip the next box up to 48 hours before your billing day, or pause for one, two, or three months. Paused plans aren't charged and resume automatically when the pause ends. Skipping doesn't change your billing date; pausing pushes it forward by the length of the pause.

My beans arrived damaged or stale — what now?

We're sorry! Reply to your dispatch email or contact us within 14 days with a photo of the bag. We'll send a free replacement on the next roast day, or refund that box if you'd prefer. Because coffee is perishable we can't accept returns of opened bags for reasons other than a fault, but freshness problems are always covered.
