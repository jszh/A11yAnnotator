Browned Butter Banana Bread

By Dana Okonkwo · Prep 15 min · Bake 55 min · Makes 1 loaf

This is the loaf I make when three bananas have gone past the point of no return. Browning the butter first gives it a nutty, almost toffee-like depth that ordinary banana bread never reaches. Below you'll find everything you need and exactly how to put it together.

Ingredients

Pull everything out before you start — the batter comes together fast once the butter is browned and you don't want to be measuring flour with one hand.

  • 3 very ripe bananas (about 1½ cups mashed)
  • 115 g (½ cup) unsalted butter
  • 150 g (¾ cup) light brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 200 g (1⅔ cups) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda · ½ tsp fine sea salt · ½ tsp cinnamon

Instructions

These steps assume a standard 9×5-inch loaf pan, greased and lined with parchment.

  1. Melt the butter in a light-coloured saucepan over medium heat, swirling, until it smells nutty and the milk solids turn golden-brown, 4–6 minutes. Pour into a large bowl and let cool 5 minutes.
  2. Whisk the brown sugar into the warm butter, then the eggs and vanilla.
  3. Mash the bananas and stir them in.
  4. In a separate bowl whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon, then fold the dry mixture into the wet just until no streaks remain.
  5. Scrape into the pan and bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 50–55 minutes, until a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.

If the top is browning too fast at the 35-minute mark, tent it loosely with foil. The steps above are the second of the recipe's two equal halves — they are not a footnote to the ingredient list.

Storage: wrapped at room temperature the loaf keeps three days, or freeze slices for up to two months.
