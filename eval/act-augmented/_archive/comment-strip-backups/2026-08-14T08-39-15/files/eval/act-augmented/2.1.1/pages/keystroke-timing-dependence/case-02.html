Map mode
Live vehicle tracking: G double-press to center on your location

Why automated checkers pass this page: The map container is focusable (tabindex="0"), has an accessible name and a role, and a documented keyboard shortcut — pressing G — is exposed in visible text. A scanner sees a reachable, named, keyboard-wired region and reports no error. What it cannot detect is that the "center on your location / live tracking" function activates only on a rapid double-press of G within 250 ms; a single press of G, or two presses spaced more than 250 ms apart, do nothing, and there is no single-press button or menu item for the same function. Users who cannot press a key twice that fast are locked out of geolocation — a second-limb SC 2.1.1 timing failure that is purely a runtime behaviour.
