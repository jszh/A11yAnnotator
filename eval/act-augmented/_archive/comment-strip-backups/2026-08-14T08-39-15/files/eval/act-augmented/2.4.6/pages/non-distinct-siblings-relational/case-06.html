Frequently asked questions

How long does standard shipping take?

Standard shipping arrives in 3–5 business days within the continental US. Tracking is emailed once your order leaves our warehouse.

What is your return policy on worn gear?

Unworn items can be returned within 60 days. Lightly worn footwear is eligible for store credit within 30 days if it shows no outdoor wear.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to Canada and the EU. Duties and import taxes are calculated at checkout and shown before you pay.

Can I change my order after placing it?

You can edit or cancel within one hour of ordering from your account page; after that the order enters fulfillment and can no longer be changed.
