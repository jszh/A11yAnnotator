Skyline tax plan splits council ahead of Friday vote

By Dana Whitmore, Municipal Affairs — 9 min read

A proposal to redirect downtown development levies toward transit operations drew sharp lines among council members Wednesday, with three votes still uncommitted hours before the measure heads to the floor.

Supporters argue the shift would stabilize bus service through the winter; opponents counter that builders will simply pass the cost to renters.
