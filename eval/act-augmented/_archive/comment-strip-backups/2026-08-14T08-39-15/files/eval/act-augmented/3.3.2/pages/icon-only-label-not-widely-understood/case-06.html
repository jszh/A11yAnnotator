Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) — Step 3 of 5.

Open to all county residents. Most applicants are seniors on fixed incomes and recently-arrived immigrant families; the form is the public's primary way to apply.

Household details

5 digits, e.g. 92501.

Built by a contractor who themed the form so "every field gets a tidy pictogram tile". The first two fields kept real text labels; for the last two the contractor grabbed glyphs from a generic symbol set (a paragraph/pilcrow mark for the count field, a Greek λ for the income-bracket field) because they "looked clean and official", and added an aria-label to each so the accessibility scanner stayed green. A sighted senior or a recently-arrived applicant sees only a ¶ and a λ — neither is widely understood by THIS audience as "household size" or "income bracket", and the two correctly-labeled fields above make the omission worse by setting the expectation that fields carry words.
