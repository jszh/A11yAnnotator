Why automated checkers pass this page: This is a standard, fully accessible login form — labeled <input> fields, a real submit <button>, visible instructions tied via aria-describedby, all keyboard reachable and operable. axe-core / WAVE / Lighthouse report no error. The barrier is invisible to them: a hidden "keystroke-dynamics" layer records the time between each PIN keypress and rejects the login if the digits are typed too slowly (the speed/rhythm of typing is itself part of authentication). A user who types correctly but slowly — or who pastes, or who uses an on-screen keyboard, switch, or speech input — is denied even with the right PIN. No untimed sign-in path is offered.
