Summer Reading Program 2026 is now open for registration

Posted by the Youth Services team · June 18, 2026 · 4 min read

We are thrilled to kick off another season of stories, prizes, and discovery. Here is everything families need to know before the program begins on July 1st.

Registration is free and open to every child and teen who lives in, attends school in, or holds a library card from the Maple Ridge district. Sign-ups can be completed at the Youth Services desk on the second floor, or online through the library account portal at any time before the closing date in August.
Readers in the Early Reader track (ages four through seven) earn a sticker for every two books a caregiver reads aloud with them, while readers in the Independent track log their own minutes and collect a small prize at each milestone of one hundred, three hundred, and six hundred minutes of reading.
Teens aged thirteen and older may instead choose the Review Challenge, in which they write a short recommendation of any title they finish; selected reviews will be printed on shelf cards in the teen collection throughout the autumn, giving fellow readers a peer-recommended place to start.

Questions? Stop by the desk any afternoon, or reply to our newsletter and a librarian will get back to you within one business day.
