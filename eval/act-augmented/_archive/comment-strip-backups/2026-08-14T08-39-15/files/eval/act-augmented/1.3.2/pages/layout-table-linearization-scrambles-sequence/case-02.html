About this work
Artist Talks

Holt painted Estuary, Low Tide over a single autumn, returning to the same mudflat at the hour the sun dropped below the headland.
Membership

She built the silver of the sky in thin glazes, letting the linen weave show through so the light seems to come from behind the surface.
Plan Your Visit

The ribbons of reflected light across the wet sand were scraped back with a palette knife in the final session, a technique she calls "drawing with absence."
Shop the Catalogue

The work anchors the gallery's argument that coastal light is less a colour than a duration — something that happens to a place over an hour.
