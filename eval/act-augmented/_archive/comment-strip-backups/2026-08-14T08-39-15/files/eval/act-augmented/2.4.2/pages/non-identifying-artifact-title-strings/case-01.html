Northwind Trading Posts Record Q3 Revenue, Driven by Logistics Segment

Published 14 October 2024 · 4 minute read

Northwind Trading Group today reported consolidated revenue of $418.6 million for the third fiscal quarter ended 30 September 2024, a 12.4% increase over the same quarter last year. Growth was led by the company's Logistics & Fulfilment segment, which benefited from new regional distribution contracts signed in July.

Management commentary

“The quarter validates the investments we made in regional warehousing,” said Chief Financial Officer Priya Raman. “We expect the Logistics segment to remain our principal growth engine through the remainder of the fiscal year.”

Outlook

The company reaffirmed full-year revenue guidance of $1.62–1.68 billion and said it would update segment-level guidance with its Q4 release in January 2025.
