Notes

  • Your legal name must match your Social Security card exactly, including hyphens and suffixes.
  • Enter your taxpayer identification number as 9 digits with no dashes or spaces (for example, 123456789, not 123-45-6789). Entries containing dashes are rejected by the IRS reporting system.
  • Nonresident aliens without an SSN should select ITIN and contact the International Student Office before continuing.
