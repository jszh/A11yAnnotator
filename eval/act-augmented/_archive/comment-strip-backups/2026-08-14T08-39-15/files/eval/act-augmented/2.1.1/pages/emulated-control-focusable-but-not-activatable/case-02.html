Slow-Braised Tuscan White Bean Ragù

This is the pot I come back to every blustery Tuesday: cannellini beans coaxed into a silky ragù with rosemary, a parmesan rind, and a long, lazy simmer. It freezes beautifully, so I always make a double batch.

Tuscan White Bean Ragù

Serves 4 · 15 min prep · 30 min cook
  • 2 × 400g tins cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 2 sticks celery, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 sprig rosemary + 1 parmesan rind
  • 400g passata · olive oil · sea salt
Save to recipe box Print this recipe

Method

  1. Sweat the onion, celery and garlic in olive oil until soft, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add the passata, rosemary and parmesan rind; simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Stir in the beans and cook gently 10 minutes more, mashing a few against the pan to thicken. Season and serve over toasted sourdough.
