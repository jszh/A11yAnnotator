Getting Started with DocForge

Welcome to DocForge. This guide walks you through creating your first space, inviting teammates, and publishing a page that the whole organisation can read. None of the steps require code, and most can be undone, so feel free to experiment as you go.

Begin by creating a space from the left sidebar. A space is simply a container for related pages — most teams start with one per project or per department. Give it a clear name, because that name appears in every breadcrumb and search result.

Once your space exists, create a page inside it and start typing. The toolbar above gives you the usual formatting controls; everything you write is saved automatically a few seconds after you stop typing, so there is no separate save step to remember.

When the page is ready, choose Publish. Until you do, only you can see the draft. After publishing, anyone with access to the space can read it, comment, and — if you allow it — suggest edits of their own.
