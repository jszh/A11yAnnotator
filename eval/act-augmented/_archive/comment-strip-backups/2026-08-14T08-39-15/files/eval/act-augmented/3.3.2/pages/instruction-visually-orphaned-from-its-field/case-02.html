Reset your security answer

For your protection, re-enter the answer to your security question exactly as you first set it. This is the final step before your online-banking sign-in is restored.

Answers are matched exactly: they are case-sensitive and any spaces you typed when you created the answer are counted, so enter it exactly as you first did (for example, "Mr Whiskers" will not match "mr whiskers" or "MrWhiskers").

After three incorrect attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes and a notice is sent to your registered email.
