Residential parking permits

Eligibility, fees, renewal, and visitor passes for permit zones A through F.

Jump to a section

  • Who is eligible
  • Fees and payment
  • Renewing a permit
  • Visitor passes

You must be a resident of a designated permit zone and the registered keeper of the vehicle. Proof of address dated within 90 days is required when you collect the permit in person.

The first permit per household is $35 per year. A second permit is $90. Visitor pass books (10 days) are $12.

Renewal opens 30 days before expiry. If your details are unchanged you can renew online; otherwise visit the Civic Center with updated proof of residence.

Each permit household may hold up to two visitor pass books at a time. Passes are scratch-to-activate and valid only within your home zone.

Report it at the Civic Center. A replacement is $15; if the vehicle was stolen, bring the police report and the replacement fee is waived.
