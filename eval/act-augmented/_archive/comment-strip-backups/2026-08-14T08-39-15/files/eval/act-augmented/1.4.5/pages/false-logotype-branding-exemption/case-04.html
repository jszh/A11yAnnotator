Apply for a heavy vehicle permit

Heavy and oversize vehicles operating on state roads need a valid permit. Permits are issued per route and are valid for twelve months. Processing currently takes up to ten working days, so apply well ahead of your travel date.

Permits for the 2026 season must be lodged before the cut-off shown in the banner above. Late applications are processed in the next cycle.
