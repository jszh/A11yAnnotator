How to repot a fiddle-leaf fig without shocking it

By Dana Okafor · Updated March 4

Fiddle-leaf figs hate surprises. Repot in spring, size up by no more than two inches of pot diameter, and keep the same soil line. Here is the gentle method we use at the nursery.

Start by watering the plant the day before — a hydrated root ball slides out of its nursery pot in one piece and resists tearing. Tip the plant sideways and coax, never yank.

Once the plant is free, tease the outer roots apart with your fingers and trim any that circle the pot. Settle it into fresh, chunky potting mix and firm the surface lightly — air pockets are the enemy.

Water thoroughly, then leave it alone for a week. Some leaf droop is normal; new growth within a month means the repot took.
