Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions our front desk hears most often.

▸ Do you offer weekend appointments?

Yes — we hold Saturday clinic hours from 9am to 1pm for vaccinations, wellness checks, and urgent concerns. Sunday is reserved for emergencies only, by phone triage first.

▸ How do I refill my pet's prescription?

Request refills through the client portal or by calling the front desk. Allow two business days for medications we compound in-house. Refrigerated items must be collected the same day.

▸ What should I bring to a first visit?

Please bring any prior medical records, a current medication list, and a stool sample if your pet is due for a parasite screen. New puppies and kittens should arrive in a secure carrier.
