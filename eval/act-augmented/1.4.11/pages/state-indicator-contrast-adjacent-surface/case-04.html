Same SC, opposite verdicts, depending on which surface is "adjacent." Group A uses a flat fill: the selected control becomes solid #1565C0, which contrasts 5.75:1 with the white page — the fill itself is the indicator, so it does not need to contrast with anything inside it (the explicitly allowed flat-design pattern). Group B puts a faint inner dot #7BB0E6 on a #4A90D9 fill (1.46:1): here the dot is the selection mark and its adjacent surface is the fill, so it fails. A naive checker that measures "control vs page" passes both; choosing the correct adjacent surface flips B to a fail.
