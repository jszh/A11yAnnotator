Renewing your vehicle registration

Answers to the questions we hear most often at DMV service counters.

You may renew up to 90 days before the expiration date printed on your current registration card. Renewing early does not shorten your new registration period — the new term still begins the day after your current one ends.

If your registration has already lapsed, you can still renew, but late fees accrue from the day after expiration. See late renewal fees below.

Most vehicles model-year 2000 and newer need a passing emissions test within 90 days of renewal. Hybrids and fully electric vehicles are exempt. Your renewal notice states whether a test is required for your specific vehicle.
