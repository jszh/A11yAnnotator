Why automated tools miss this: All ten wordmarks are text glyphs styled as company logos on a black page. The first nine are set in #1c1c1c (~1.23:1) and only become white on hover — an author choice to make the wall look sleek, so the logo/Essential exemption does NOT save them: they FAIL 1.4.11. The tenth, BNØRD, is set in its brand-mandated navy #11203a and is therefore Essential and exempt (NA). A contrast tool cannot tell brand-mandated color from author styling, nor whether a hover reveal is "supplemental" or a cover for a too-faint default. Both are wordmarks at low contrast; only a human knowing brand intent can separate the FAIL set from the NA one.
