How the challenge works

Every summer, children across the borough take part in our reading challenge. The idea is simple: borrow and read six library books between July and September, collect a sticker for each one, and earn a certificate and a small prize at the finish line.

Reading over the holidays helps children keep up the progress they made during the school year. Our librarians are on hand to recommend books for every reader, from first picture books to chapter-book adventures, and there are themed events most Saturdays throughout the summer.

Joining is free and open to all children aged four to fourteen. Parents and carers can sign up their child at the desk of any branch, or online using the link below.
