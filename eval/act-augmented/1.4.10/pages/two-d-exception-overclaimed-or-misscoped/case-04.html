Hartwell Family Dental

Gentle, modern dentistry for every generation — established 1998.

For over twenty-five years our family-owned practice has cared for the families of Hartwell and the surrounding villages. We believe dentistry should be calm, unhurried, and tailored to you. Below you can meet the people who look after your smile and read about the everyday and specialist treatments we provide.

Meet the team & what we offer

Dr. Aisha Hartwell

Principal Dentist, BDS

Aisha founded the practice after training in Leeds. She has a particular interest in nervous patients and minimally invasive restorative work, and she still does the school visits that started the practice’s outreach programme.

Marcus Vale

Hygienist & Therapist

Marcus leads our preventive care. Whether it is a routine clean, gum-health coaching, or helping children build good habits early, he keeps appointments relaxed and explains every step before he begins.

General & family care

Everyday treatments

Check-ups, fillings, crowns, and emergency appointments for the whole family. We hold same-day slots each morning for urgent pain, and we never charge a consultation fee for children under twelve.

Cosmetic & specialist

When you want more

Teeth whitening, clear aligners, and implant referrals through our partner specialists. We will always talk you through the options, costs, and what to realistically expect before anything is booked.

New patients are warmly welcome. Call the front desk or use our online booking form, and a member of the team will help you find a time that suits your family.
