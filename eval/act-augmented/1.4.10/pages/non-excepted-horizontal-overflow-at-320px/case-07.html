Metro Transit — Route 12 (Downtown ↔ University)

Weekday timetable, effective June 2026

Before you ride

Route 12 runs every twenty minutes on weekdays between Downtown Transit Center and the University Quad, with extra trips added during the morning and afternoon peaks. All buses on this route are low-floor and wheelchair accessible, and bikes are welcome on the front rack on a first-come basis. Service notes that affect today's trips are listed below the timetable, so please read all the way to the bottom before you plan your trip.

Service notes

Through the end of June, the Civic Center stop is temporarily relocated one block north to 7th & Main because of streetscape construction. Buses will still serve the area at roughly the same times shown above, but please watch for the temporary stop sign rather than the permanent shelter. Real-time arrivals continue to update normally during the relocation.

On days when the University holds commencement, Route 12 detours around the Quad and terminates at Maple Avenue. We post these detours by 5 a.m. on the morning they take effect, both here and on the rider alerts page, so check before you head out during graduation week.
