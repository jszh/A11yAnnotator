Cascade Ridge Trailhead

June conditions on the Ridge Loop

Last updated this morning by the volunteer trail crew.

The snow line has finally retreated above the second switchback, which means the full Ridge Loop is walkable again for the first time since October. Expect soft, muddy tread in the shaded sections below the saddle for at least another week as the ground keeps thawing.

The creek crossing at mile 3.1 is running fast and cold with snowmelt. The footlog is in place and stable, but it is slick in the mornings; trekking poles are strongly recommended and small children should be carried across rather than walked.

Wildflowers are just beginning along the upper meadow, and the marmots are out and unbothered. Please stay on the established tread through the meadow — the alpine soil takes decades to recover from a single boot print off-trail.

Permits are required for overnight use between June and September and can be reserved online up to two weeks ahead. Day use does not require a permit, but the parking lot fills by 8 a.m. on weekends.
