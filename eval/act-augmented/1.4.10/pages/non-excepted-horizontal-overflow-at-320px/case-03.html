Frequently asked questions

What are the current branch hours?

The Central branch is open Monday through Thursday from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday and Saturday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed on Sundays and county holidays. Neighborhood branches keep shorter hours, so please check each branch page before you visit.

How long can I keep a hold once it arrives?

When an item you requested is ready, we hold it on the pickup shelf for seven calendar days. You will receive an email or text the moment it is available. If you cannot make it in time, you can renew the hold once from your account before it expires.

Do you still charge late fees?

We went fine-free for most materials in 2023. You will no longer be charged a daily overdue fee on books, audiobooks, or magazines. We do still charge a replacement cost if an item is more than thirty days overdue and is presumed lost, but that charge is removed the moment the item is returned in good condition.

Can I use my library card at any branch?

Yes. One card works across every branch in the county system, and you can pick up holds at whichever location is most convenient for you. You can change your default pickup branch at any time from the account settings page.
