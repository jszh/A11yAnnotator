Harborlight Foundation — Annual Report 2025

Strengthening coastal communities since 1994

A Letter from the Director

Reflecting on a year of storms, rebuilding, and the people who made it possible.

Dear friends and supporters,

When I look back on this past year, the word that keeps returning to me is resilience. We began the spring still repairing the seawall in Otter Cove, and we ended the autumn handing the keys to fourteen rebuilt homes. None of that was inevitable. It happened because thousands of you decided that a coastline is worth fighting for.

Our grantmaking grew by nineteen percent, but the number I am proudest of is smaller and harder to put on a chart: the four hundred volunteers who showed up, again and again, in the rain, with shovels and patience.

We also learned hard lessons. The flooding in March moved faster than our early-warning network could keep up with, and we have spent the months since rebuilding that system from the ground up so that no family is caught unaware again.

In the year ahead we will double our investment in flood resilience, expand the small-grants program to two new counties, and finally launch the apprenticeship fund that so many of you have asked about.

Thank you for walking this shoreline with us.

— Dolores Marchetti, Executive Director
