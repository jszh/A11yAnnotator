How a message travels through Pollen

Before opening a pull request that touches routing, it helps to understand the path a single message takes from a producer to its subscribers. The diagram below shows the fan-out: one Ingress hands a message to the Router, which writes it to a durable log and then notifies every matching subscriber group in parallel.

Topology

The arrows show direction of flow; the alignment of each branch shows which component talks to which. The two subscriber groups receive copies independently.
