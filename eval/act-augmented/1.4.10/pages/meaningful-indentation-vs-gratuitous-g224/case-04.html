About this outline

Selecting a provision opens its full text and annotations. The outline is intentionally indented so you can see the nesting — for example, that subsection § 482.13(a) sits inside section § 482.13, which sits inside Part 482. That containment is meaningful, so the tree is not flattened.

The container scrolls sideways so a nested level can be brought into view. However, each outline item's text is held on a single line, so once a level is in view a reader at a narrow width must still scroll the row left and right to read the whole provision — the sentence runs past the right edge instead of wrapping into the column.
