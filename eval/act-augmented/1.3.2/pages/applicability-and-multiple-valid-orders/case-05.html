Assembling the Lindholm shelf bracket

Four steps, about ten minutes. Read each step fully before you start it.

●

Hang the shelf

Then lower the shelf board onto the two bracket arms so the pre-drilled holes drop over the pins, and slide it back until the board meets the wall.

●

Tighten and check

Finally, tighten the two grub screws under the shelf with the hex key until the board no longer lifts, and check it is level before loading it.

●

Attach the wall plate

First, hold the steel wall plate against the marked line, drill the two upper holes, and drive in the supplied wall anchors and screws so the plate sits flush.

●

Insert the bracket pins

Next, screw the two bracket arms into the threaded sockets on the wall plate until the locating pins point straight out and the arms are fully seated.

You will need

  • Cordless drill and a 6 mm masonry bit
  • The supplied hex key and wall anchors
  • A spirit level and a pencil
