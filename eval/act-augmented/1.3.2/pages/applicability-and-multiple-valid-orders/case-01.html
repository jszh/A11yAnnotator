Members’ notice

Subscriptions are due by 30 April

From the Treasurer, R. Akhtar

Annual membership stands at £18 (concessions £9) and falls due at the start of the new recording year. You can renew by standing order, by cheque to the society’s post box, or in person at the April indoor meeting.

Members who renew before the deadline keep their existing recorder number and the early-bird discount on the summer ringing course. Lapsed members are removed from the county distribution list and must re-apply for survey access.

Any queries about gift-aid declarations should go to the treasurer directly rather than the general inbox, which is no longer monitored daily.

The return of the curlew to Holm Marsh

By Eleanor Pryce, county wader recorder

For the first time since the 2011 floods, breeding curlew have been confirmed on the restored grazing marsh at Holm. Three displaying pairs were logged across the April transects, and at least one nest has now reached the incubation stage under the fenced exclosure on the north compartment.

The recovery follows four winters of raised water levels and the removal of two drainage culverts that had been quietly dewatering the southern fields. Wet ground in March is what curlew need: it keeps the soil soft enough for chicks to probe for invertebrates in their first fragile fortnight.

Volunteers are asked to keep to the boardwalk between now and July and to report any loose dogs to the warden. A full breeding-season summary, with productivity figures, will appear in the autumn bulletin.
