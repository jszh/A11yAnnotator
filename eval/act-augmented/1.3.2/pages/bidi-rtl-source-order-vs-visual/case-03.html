Side-by-side — visual order vs. logical (source) order

What the curator sees: On loan from the الأرشيف 2023 collection, restored last spring.
What a screen reader reads (source order): “On loan from the 2023 al-Arshif collection…” — the acquisition year is announced before the exhibit name because the two tokens were swapped in the byte stream to make the bidi rendering look right, instead of wrapping the Arabic run in <bdi>.

Why automated tools miss it: the page is valid lang="en" HTML, every attribute is present, and the on-screen rendering is correct — nothing looks wrong. Only a human who reads Arabic, knows it is RTL, and compares the year’s logical position to its visual position can detect that two tokens were transposed in the source to satisfy the bidirectional algorithm. axe, WAVE, and Lighthouse perform no multilingual logical-vs-visual order comparison.
