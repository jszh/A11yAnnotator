Brown-Butter Madeleines

Crisp shell, tender hump, hazelnut aroma. Makes 12.

Prep 20 min · Chill 1 hr · Bake 9 min

Method

  1. 4Pipe the batter into a buttered madeleine mold and bake at 200 C for 9 minutes, until the humps spring back. Cool 2 minutes before unmolding.
  2. 3Fold the sifted flour into the egg mixture in three additions, then stream in the cooled brown butter. Rest the batter, covered, for 1 hour.
  3. 2Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and tripled in volume, about 4 minutes. Add the vanilla and a pinch of salt.
  4. 1Brown the butter in a light-colored pan over medium heat until it smells of hazelnuts and the milk solids turn amber. Let it cool to lukewarm.
