Caminante, no hay camino

Antonio Machado (1875–1939) · with a facing English translation

Español English
Caminante, son tus huellas Wanderer, your footprints are
el camino y nada más; the road and nothing more;
caminante, no hay camino, wanderer, there is no road,
se hace camino al andar. the road is made by walking.

From Proverbios y cantares (XXIX), Campos de Castilla, 1912. Translation prepared for the parallel-text reading group.
