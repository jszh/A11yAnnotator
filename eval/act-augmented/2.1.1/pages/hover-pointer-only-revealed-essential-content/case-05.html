Brown Butter Banana Bread

Prep 15 min·Bake 55 min·Makes 1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks unsalted butter, browned and cooled
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 cup packed dark brown sugar
  • 2 eggs, room temperature
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Heat oven to 175 °C and line a loaf tin with parchment.
  2. Brown the butter in a light pan until it smells nutty and the milk solids turn amber; cool 10 minutes.
  3. Whisk the bananas, brown sugar, eggs, browned butter, and vanilla until glossy.
  4. Fold in the flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined — do not overmix.
  5. Pour into the tin and bake 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.
