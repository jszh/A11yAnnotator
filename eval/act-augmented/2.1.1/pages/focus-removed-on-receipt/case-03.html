City Council Approves Transit Levy After Marathon Session

By Dana Okafor · Updated 6:14 a.m.

The council voted 7–4 early Wednesday to place a half-cent transit levy before voters in November, ending a debate that stretched past midnight and drew an overflow crowd to the chamber.

Supporters argued the measure would stabilize bus service in the east end, where routes were cut twice last year. Opponents, including two members who represent suburban wards, said the city had not exhausted other funding options.

The levy now heads to the county elections office for certification. If approved by voters, collection would begin in the next fiscal year, officials said.
