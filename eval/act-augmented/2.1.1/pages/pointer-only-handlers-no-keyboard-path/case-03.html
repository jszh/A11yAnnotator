In pictures: the harbour at dawn

By Marisol Vance · Photographs by the Courier picture desk · 14 March

View all 8 photos in a single list ↓

The trawlers slip their moorings before the first light reaches the breakwater. For three generations the Penhallow family has worked this stretch of coast, and the pre-dawn ritual has barely changed: nets checked by torchlight, flasks filled, the diesel cough of an engine carrying across still water.

Our picture desk spent a week documenting the fleet. The full set of eight images is published below the slideshow for readers who prefer to scroll.
